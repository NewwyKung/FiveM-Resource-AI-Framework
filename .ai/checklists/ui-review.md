# UI Review Gate

- Wireframe is Approved before visual implementation begins.
- Primary task and information hierarchy are obvious.
- Layout, spacing, typography, and component states match the specification.
- Keyboard, focus, Escape, and close recovery work.
- Loading, empty, dense, error, disabled, and destructive states are covered.
- Thai and long-content cases do not clip or overlap.
- UI remains readable over bright and dark gameplay backgrounds.
- Motion is purposeful, interruptible, and reduced-motion safe.
- Assets are optimized and dynamic text is not embedded in raster images.
- Screenshots and unperformed checks are attached to the review result.
